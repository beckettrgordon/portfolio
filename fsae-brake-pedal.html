FSAE Brake Pedal System

Beckett Gordon
September 22, 2026
Updated on September 27, 2026

Goals

This brake pedal & carriage design was inherited from GT's 2026 FSAE car, and my goal was to validate the design under new load cases. I worked on analysis of brake pedal and carriage using SolidWorks and Ansys, and derived load cases for floor analysis.

Load Cases

FSAE rules require that the brake pedal system must withstand a force of 2000 N "panic braking" applied to the pedal. If one master cylinder fails mid-drive, the forces on the carriage increase, so this was also considered.
